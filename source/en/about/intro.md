## 1. Overview

The Sci-Tech Innovation Club of Yichang No.1 High School is rooted in the school's profound tradition of science and technology education. Upholding the educational philosophy of "**Self-Education, Lifelong Development**", the school has built a distinctive science and innovation curriculum system, and stimulates students' innovative thinking through the sci-tech club, the Science & Technology Festival and the Mathematics & Science Culture Festival.
